# LemonTodo Trademark Guidelines

LemonTodo names, logos, icons, service names, and related branding are not
licensed by the source code licenses.

You may truthfully state that your project is based on LemonTodo source code,
provided the statement is not misleading and does not imply official
endorsement.

You may not use LemonTodo branding for an unofficial hosted service, fork,
commercial offering, or distribution in a way that suggests it is provided,
approved, or sponsored by the LemonTodo project.

These guidelines are a project placeholder and require legal review before a
public release.
